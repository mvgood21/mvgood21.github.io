How the apps are made

  • Photos and documents stay on the device

    The apps that handle photos, videos and documents (Compress Image, Flip & Rotate Image, Convert Image, SugarAlbum, HWP Viewer SugarDoc) process files on the device without uploading them, and never modify or delete the originals.

  • No sign-up

    None of the ten public apps has a sign-up or log-in screen. SugarSpeller creates an anonymous ID, not linked to any name or email, to count daily use of AI explanations.

  • Policies that match the code

    Each app's privacy policy is written from its actual code, including what the ad and analytics SDKs send.

Frequently asked questions

Who makes SugarMount apps?

SugarMount is the developer name used by simpest, an independent developer, to publish apps on Google Play. Planning, development and support are all done by the same person. About SugarMount

Are my photos or documents uploaded?

Compress Image, Flip & Rotate Image, Convert Image, HWP Viewer SugarDoc and SugarAlbum process files on your device without uploading them. SugarSpeller sends the text you check to the Naver or Daum spelling checker. Each app's privacy policy has the details.

Are the apps free?

All apps are free to install. Some show ads and offer ad removal or Pro features as a one-time purchase or subscription. HWP Viewer SugarDoc has no ads.

How do I get support or report a bug?

Email sugarmount21@gmail.com. Including the app name, device model and Android version helps us check faster.
