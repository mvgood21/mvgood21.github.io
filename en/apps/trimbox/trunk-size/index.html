Why app volume differs from the brochure

Brochure trunk volumes (VDA) are usually measured with 1-liter blocks up to the cover or window line, which is not how camping gear is packed. The app volume is the space from the floor behind the 2nd row up to the ceiling, where the tailgate still closes, in the app's trunk shape. It comes out larger than the brochure figure, and two trunks with the same liters can still take different loads because of wheel arches, a sloped tailgate and a dropping ceiling.

Kia Sorento trunk size (MQ4)

The Sorento MQ4 (2021+ model year in the US) 5-seat and 7-seat with the 3rd row folded share the same space above the floor. The floor is 109 cm between the wheel arches and 107 cm deep, widening to 138 cm only in the last 38 cm by the tailgate. Height is 82 cm, 79 cm at the opening. You can also choose the 2nd row slid 13 cm or 27 cm forward.

Width and depth are checked against published tape measurements; height, wheel-arch length and tailgate slope are estimates. The app volume of about 951 L is a little below ADAC's 980 to 1,000 L measured to the ceiling behind the 2nd row. All 16 items of the family set fit with the 2nd row all the way back.

Hyundai Santa Fe trunk size (MX5)

The Santa Fe MX5 (2024+ model year) 5-seat and 7-seat with the 3rd row folded share the same space above the floor and differ only in under-floor storage. The floor is 108 × 104 cm and the height 87 cm, 82 cm at the opening: a little shorter and taller than the Sorento. The opening is about 127 cm wide at the floor.

The app volume of about 970 L is matched to ADAC's 975 L measured to the ceiling behind the 2nd row. There is no 2nd-row slide option. All 16 family items fit.

Hyundai Palisade trunk size (LX3)

The Palisade LX3 (2026+ model year) is included as a 7-seat with the 3rd row folded. Its 115 × 122 cm floor is the longest of the supported SUVs. Height is 84 cm, 80 cm at the opening.

With no official interior dimensions, the shape is matched to about 1,140 L: the US 3rd-row-folded volume (SAE 1,311 L) scaled by the same ratio as the Sorento. All 16 family items fit.

Kia Carnival trunk size (KA4)

The Carnival KA4 has three setups. 3rd row up means loading into the well behind the rear bench (floor 127 × 68 cm). The opening's sill is about 40 cm above the well floor, and you can stack up to 150 cm. The well is short, so 15 of the 16 family items fit and a storage box is left over.

7-seat, 3rd row stowed gives a flat floor of 127 × 145 cm (about 2,006 L), with the 2nd row slid 6 cm or 12 cm forward as options. 9-seat, 4th row stowed uses the 3rd-row seats as the front wall, so the floor is 110 cm deep (about 1,589 L; no official figure to compare). All 16 family items fit in both. With no official interior dimensions, these shapes are worked back from official volumes (VDA and SAE).

Kia Sportage trunk size (NQ5)

This is the long-wheelbase Sportage NQ5 sold in Korea and North America. The floor is 104 × 94 cm and the height 80 cm, 73 cm at the opening. Published tape measurements exist only for the shorter European body, so the length is estimated from that. The app volume is about 707 L.

14 of the 16 family items fit; the 50 L cooler and one sleeping bag are left over.

Tesla Model Y trunk size (2025+)

The Model Y (2025+) is 94 cm between the wheel arches, 110 cm deep and 80 cm high. The back slopes down, so the higher you stack, the narrower it gets toward the tailgate.

The app volume of about 623 L is above ADAC's block-measured 540 L to the ceiling (blocks fill less of the space under the sloped glass). The deep under-floor well (about 105 L by ADAC) and the front trunk are not counted yet. 12 of the 16 family items fit; the tent, the large tarp, the roll table and one chair are left over. With the under-floor well you may fit more in the real car.

Hyundai IONIQ 5 trunk size (2024+)

The IONIQ 5 facelift (2024+) has a 105 × 100 cm floor with the 2nd row all the way back, a height of 80 cm, 71 cm at the opening, and a 3 cm sill. You can also choose the 2nd row slid 7 cm or 14 cm forward.

The app volume of about 660 L is about 10% above ADAC's 600 L to the ceiling behind the 2nd row. With the 2nd row back, 12 of the 16 family items fit (the tent, the large tarp, the roll table and the 50 L cooler are left over); with the 2nd row all the way forward (about 14 cm), 15 fit. The front trunk is not counted.

Frequently asked questions

Are these official dimensions?

No. Carmakers rarely publish trunk length and width. The Sorento values are checked against published tape measurements; the others are estimated from official volumes, body specs and published measurements, and may differ from your car by a few centimeters.

My own measurements are different.

Send your tape measurements to sugarmount21@gmail.com and we will consider them for the next version. Please say what you measured from where (floor width, between the wheel arches, depth, opening height).

My car isn't on the list.

Only these 7 vehicles for now. Any other car can be entered as a box by its trunk width, depth and height. Tap Request a vehicle in the vehicle menu; we add vehicles in order of requests.

Does it support SUVs with the 3rd row up?

SUVs are 5-seat or 7-seat with the 3rd row folded. Only the Carnival is included with the 3rd row up.
