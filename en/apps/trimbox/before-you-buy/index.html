Fit it in the trunk before you buy it

Will this tent still fit with everything else? Before you check out, put the new gear into your trunk next to what you already pack, and see whether it all fits.

    It says "15 of 16 items fit" and lists what is left by name, plus how many a repack would fit.

  • Tall gear and the tailgate

    Tall coolers and boxes tend to hit the sloped tailgate. If one does, it turns red and shows how far it sticks out.

Check before you buy

  1. Load what you already take: add your usual camping gear, or open a saved layout from My layouts.
  2. Add the new item: find it among 197 camping items, or use Custom and enter the packed size from the product page (folded, not set up).
  3. Tap Will it fit?: see whether everything fits, or what is left over and why. Try Repack, and on the Sorento, IONIQ 5 or Carnival 7-seat it can suggest sliding the 2nd row a few centimeters.

You can also try the same gear in another car. Switch vehicles and your gear stays, so you can compare your current car and your next one with the same load.

Good to know

  • Every item is treated as a box of its width, depth and height, so round bags and folding chairs may take a little more room than in real life.
  • Sleeping bags and soft bags are squeezed only as much as needed. Listed coolers and heaters that must stay upright are kept upright.
  • Weights are estimates from product data or typical values for the item type.
  • The Sorento is checked against published tape measurements; the other six vehicles are estimated from specs and may be off by a few centimeters.

Frequently asked questions

The item I want isn't listed.

Open Custom in the gear picker and enter a name, width, depth and height. Use the packed size from the product page, the size in its carry bag.

Do I have to add my usual gear every time?

No. Your current layout saves automatically, and you can save named layouts. Named saves start with 1 slot and gain one more for each short ad you watch, up to 5. Pro (one-time purchase) has no slot limit.

I'm also thinking about a new car.

Switch the vehicle and repack; your gear stays. Apart from the Sorento, the trunk shapes are estimated from specs, so check a real trunk before you decide on a car.

Does it need internet?

The check, auto-pack and saving work without internet. Only ads, usage analytics and the Pro purchase use it.

Trunk first, then checkout.

Try your usual gear and the new item in your car together.
