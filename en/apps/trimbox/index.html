TrimBox

TrimBox is an Android app that packs your camping gear into the 3D trunk of one of 7 SUVs and minivans and shows whether it all fits and the tailgate closes.

Coming soon TrimBox: Car Trunk Packing 3D

TrimBox privacy policy

Use it to

  • Find out before a camping trip whether everything fits in one go
  • Decide what to load first: tent, cooler, chairs or table
  • Check whether new gear fits alongside what you already pack, before buying it
  • Compare your current car and your next one with the same gear

What it does

  • 7 SUVs and minivans

    Kia Sorento (MQ4) 5-seat and 7-seat with the 3rd row folded, Hyundai Santa Fe (MX5) 5-seat and 7-seat with the 3rd row folded, Hyundai Palisade (LX3) 7-seat with the 3rd row folded, Kia Carnival (KA4) with the 3rd row up or the rear rows stowed (7-seat or 9-seat), Kia Sportage (NQ5), Tesla Model Y (2025+) and Hyundai IONIQ 5 (2024+). Each one has its own trunk size, wheel arches, sloped tailgate, seatback angle and rear view. The Sorento is checked against published tape measurements; the other six are estimated from specs and official cargo volumes, and the vehicle menu marks each one Measured or Estimated.

  • One tap: Will it fit?

    Tells you whether the current layout fits as it is, and if not, how many items are blocked and why: wheel arch, ceiling, tailgate, seatback or another item. It shows how many a repack would fit and applies that layout with one tap.

  • See if the tailgate closes

    If the top of a load hits the sloped tailgate, it turns red and shows how many centimeters stick out. If not everything fits, it suggests how far to slide the 2nd row forward (Sorento, IONIQ 5, Carnival 7-seat).

  • Just pick. It packs itself.

    Gear goes into the trunk as you add it: big items low and deep, long items lying down, small items in the gaps, with the wheel arches used as shelves. Items of 20 kg or more stay on the floor, things you reach for often, like a cooler or first-aid kit, go near the tailgate, and soft bags are squeezed only as much as needed. It never returns a layout with floating or overlapping items.

  • Loading order, step by step

    Every item gets a number in the order to load it, and you can step through them one at a time. Tips point out heavy items on top of light ones and everyday items buried deep inside.

  • 197 camping items

    Packed sizes of tents, tarps, tables, chairs, coolers, sleeping bags, mats, stoves, storage boxes and car camping gear, plus 8 suitcases and 5 moving boxes. Search and set a quantity, or add a whole set: Minimal for 2, Family of 4 or Solo backpacking. Enter the size of anything that is missing.

  • Same gear, another car

    Switch vehicles and your gear stays. Repack and tap Will it fit? to compare your current car and your next one with the same load.

  • Save & share card

    One card with the layout and the numbered loading order, saved to your gallery or shared with your family. It takes one short ad per layout; if no ad loads, the card is made anyway.

  • Move things yourself, and save

    Rotate and zoom the 3D trunk, drag items, rotate or delete them, undo and redo. Start over clears the gear and keeps the vehicle. Your work saves automatically. Named saves start with 1 slot and gain one more for each short ad you watch (up to 5), and opening a save never needs an ad. You can also export a layout as a file and import it again.

  • Pro (one-time purchase)

    With Pro, ads stop showing everywhere in the app and named saves are unlimited. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription; after reinstalling or on a new device, tap Restore purchase in About with the same Google account. Vehicles, the check, auto-pack and the loading guide all work without Pro.

Data and permissions

  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. The ads SDK shares the advertising ID and app set ID, approximate location estimated from the IP address, app interactions and diagnostics with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland an ad consent screen (Google UMP) appears, and you can change your choice any time in Privacy options in About. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.
  • Google Play handles the Pro purchase. The app never receives card numbers or other payment details; it turns Pro on from the purchase state Google Play reports and keeps only a Pro yes/no flag on your phone. Once you have Pro, the ads SDK is not started when you open the app.
  • Only the ads SDK and Google Play billing use the internet. There is no account or sign-in, and no analytics or crash reporting SDK.
  • Layouts, their names and the number of save slots you have unlocked are stored only on your phone and are never put into ad requests.
  • Save & share card, layout file export and import, and feedback email happen only when you tap them, through Android's share sheet, photo storage, file picker or your mail app. The Google Play link in the shared text carries only a tag that marks the visit as coming from a share (utm_source=trimbox_share), no personal data. The error log stays on your phone and goes into a feedback email only if you choose to include it.

See TrimBox privacy policy for details.

What it can't do

  • The six vehicles other than the Sorento are estimated from specs and official cargo volumes and may differ from your car by a few centimeters. Some Sorento values, such as height and tailgate slope, are estimates too. Leave some room for tight fits.
  • Every item is treated as a box of its width, depth and height. Under-floor storage, SUVs with the 3rd row up and the luggage cover are not modeled.
  • It does not work out sleeping space inside the car, only the gear you load.
  • Weights are estimates from product data or typical values for the item type.
  • A layout holds up to 64 items.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check whether all my camping gear fits?

Pick your gear and it is placed in the trunk right away. Tap Will it fit? to see whether the current layout fits, what is blocked and why, and how many items a repack would fit. It also checks whether the tailgate will close.

How close is it to my real car?

The Sorento is checked against published tape measurements, though a few values such as height and tailgate slope are estimates. The other six vehicles are estimated from specs and official cargo volumes and may be off by a few centimeters. Treat the result as a guide, leave room for tight fits, and tell us at sugarmount21@gmail.com if a size looks wrong for your car.

Which cars does it support?

Kia Sorento (MQ4), Hyundai Santa Fe (MX5), Hyundai Palisade (LX3), Kia Carnival (KA4), Kia Sportage (NQ5), Tesla Model Y (2025+) and Hyundai IONIQ 5 (2024+). For any other car, enter the trunk width, depth and height (wheel arches optional) to get a box shape. Ask for your car with Request a vehicle in the vehicle menu or in About.

Is it free?

It is free to install and shows ads. Vehicles, the check, auto-pack, the loading guide and the save & share card all work without paying. Named saves start with 1 slot and gain one more for each short ad you watch, up to 5.

What does Pro change?

With Pro (a one-time purchase), app open, rewarded and banner ads all stop showing, the card is made right away with no ad to watch, and named saves are unlimited. It is not a subscription, so nothing renews.

Are my layouts sent anywhere?

No. Layouts and their names stay on your phone, and we run no servers. The only data that leaves the device is the ad data the ads SDK sends to Google and the Google Play purchase; cards, layout files and emails leave only when you share or send them. The check and auto-pack work without internet.

When do ads appear?

Never while checking, packing or following the loading steps. An app open ad may appear when you come back after a while (max 2 a day), rewarded ads play only when you choose one for a card or a save slot, and there is a banner at the bottom of My layouts.
