Check the trunk before you buy a roof box

Whether you need a roof box depends on how much of your gear fits in the trunk. Pack your camping gear into the trunk on your phone first, and see what is left over.

  • Leftovers, right away

    If the trunk can't take everything, the items that don't fit are listed by name. Those are the ones that need the roof or somewhere else.

  • Shape, not just liters

    Two trunks with the same liters can take different loads because of wheel arches, a sloped tailgate and a dropping ceiling. Each vehicle has its own shape.

  • Stack high, and still close

    Stacking up to fill the trunk is where loads hit the sloped tailgate. Anything that does turns red, with how far it sticks out.

A roof box decision, in this order

  1. Add the whole trip's gear: tents, tarps, tables, chairs, coolers and sleeping bags from the list, or a ready-made set.
  2. Tap Will it fit?: if everything fits, the trunk is enough. If not, see what is left over.
  3. Try Repack and the 2nd-row tip: see whether a new layout or sliding the 2nd row a little solves it (Sorento, IONIQ 5, Carnival 7-seat).
  4. Still left over?: use the size and number of the leftovers to judge how much extra space you need.

TrimBox only counts the inside of the trunk. It does not model roof boxes, roof bags or trunk organizers themselves. The Sorento is checked against published tape measurements; the other six vehicles are estimated from specs and may be off by a few centimeters.

Frequently asked questions

Does it tell me what size roof box to buy?

No. Roof boxes are not modeled. It shows which items don't fit in the trunk and how big they are, so you can choose a roof box from that.

What about trunk organizers or folding crates?

Folding crates and storage boxes are on the list, and any other organizer can be added as a box by its width, depth and height. Small things that go inside it don't need to be added.

Does sliding the 2nd row really help?

On the Sorento, IONIQ 5 and Carnival 7-seat you can choose a forward 2nd-row position and check again, and the result sheet may suggest how many centimeters would fit everything. Check that the rear seats still have room in the real car.

Does it cost anything?

It is free to install and shows ads. Nothing is shown while you pick gear, pack, check or follow the loading steps.
