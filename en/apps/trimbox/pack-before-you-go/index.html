Pack the car on your phone before you pack the car

Will all of this fit in the trunk? Find out at home, not in the parking lot. TrimBox packs your camping gear into the shape of your trunk and tells you whether the tailgate will close.

  • See if the tailgate closes

    If the top of the load hits the sloped tailgate, it turns red and shows how many centimeters stick out.

  • See what's left over

    Items that don't fit are listed by name, with how many a repack would fit and whether sliding the 2nd row would do it.

  • Know what to load first

    Every item gets a number. Heavy things go low, and the cooler you reach for often ends up near the tailgate.

How it works

  1. Pick your car: Kia Sorento, Hyundai Santa Fe, Hyundai Palisade, Kia Carnival, Kia Sportage, Tesla Model Y or Hyundai IONIQ 5. For any other car, enter the trunk width, depth and height to get a box shape.
  2. Add your gear: choose from 197 camping items (tents, tarps, tables, chairs, coolers, sleeping bags) or add a whole set: Minimal for 2, Family of 4 or Solo backpacking. Each item goes into the trunk as you add it.
  3. Tap Will it fit?: see whether the layout fits as it is, and if not, which items are blocked and why.
  4. Load in order: step through the loading guide one item at a time. The layout saves itself, so it is there again when you pack up to go home.

Show it instead of explaining it

Turn the layout and the numbered loading order into one card and send it to whoever is helping you pack.

Sizes can differ from your car. The Sorento is checked against published tape measurements, though some values such as height and tailgate slope are estimates. The other six vehicles are estimated from specs and official cargo volumes, so they may be off by a few centimeters. Leave some room for tight fits. Under-floor storage and the Model Y and IONIQ 5 front trunks are not counted yet.

Frequently asked questions

Does it need internet?

The check, auto-pack and saving work without internet. Only ads, usage analytics and the Pro purchase use it, so you can open a saved layout at a campsite with a weak signal.

Does it cost anything?

It is free to install and shows ads. Nothing is shown while you pick gear, pack, check or follow the loading steps. Pro, a one-time purchase rather than a subscription, removes the ads and makes named saves unlimited.

My car isn't on the list.

Enter the trunk width, depth and height (wheel arches optional) to see it as a box. Tap Request a vehicle at the bottom of the vehicle menu; we add vehicles in order of requests.

Are my layouts sent anywhere?

No. Layouts and their names stay on your phone, and we run no servers. Cards and layout files leave only when you share them. See the TrimBox privacy policy.
