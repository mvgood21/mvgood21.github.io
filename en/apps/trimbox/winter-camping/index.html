Why winter loads overflow

Winter gear is bulkier for the same number of people. A heater has to stand upright, and winter sleeping bags and thick mats take more room. Two trunks with the same liters can take different loads because of the wheel arches, the sloped tailgate and a ceiling that drops toward the back.

TrimBox packs into each vehicle's own trunk shape. Items that must stay upright, like heaters, fuel cans and coolers, are kept upright, items of 20 kg or more stay on the floor, and soft items like sleeping bags are squeezed only as much as needed.

How to check a winter load

  1. Open your usual load: open a layout saved from your last trip, or start from a set such as Family of 4.
  2. Add the winter gear: add a heater, a fuel can, the heated blanket and sleeping bags from the list. Use Custom only for anything that is not listed.
  3. Tap Will it fit?: see whether it all fits, what is left over and whether the tailgate closes. Save the layout and it is ready again when you pack up.

Sizes can differ from your car. The Sorento is checked against published tape measurements; the other six vehicles are estimated from specs and official cargo volumes. Under-floor storage is not counted. TrimBox does not judge how to carry fuel or a heater in a car; follow the maker's instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Which heaters and fuel cans are listed?

A large 9 L kerosene fan heater, kerosene heaters (large round 7 L, large square 7.5 L and a small camping one) and kerosene cans (20 L and 10 L, weighed full). If yours is a very different size, enter its outside size including the handle in Custom.

Will it lay the heater on its side?

Listed heaters, fuel cans and coolers are always kept upright. Items you enter yourself may be laid down to fit, so check the result if yours must stand.

Can I use it when packing up at the campsite?

Yes. Layouts stay on your phone, and the check, auto-pack and saving work without internet. Only ads and the Pro purchase use it.

Which cars does it support?

Kia Sorento, Hyundai Santa Fe, Hyundai Palisade, Kia Carnival, Kia Sportage, Tesla Model Y and Hyundai IONIQ 5. Any other car can be entered as a box. See trunk sizes by vehicle.
