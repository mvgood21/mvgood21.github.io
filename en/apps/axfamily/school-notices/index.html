School notices and class schedules in one calendar

Kids' plans arrive on paper notices, in school apps and as texts from after-school classes. Here is how to pull out the dates and things to bring, put them in one family calendar, and decide who handles each.

By SugarMount · 5 min read · Last updated November 22, 2026

This guide is based on AXFamily 1.2.0, an Android app by SugarMount. Every school, name and event in the text and screens is made up. AXFamily isn't made by a school, so you add the notices you receive yourself.

When only the person who got the notice knows what's in it, the others easily miss a reply deadline or something to bring. One habit is enough: whoever receives it adds it right away and picks who takes care of it.

Turn a school notice photo into events

  1. Add — Tap Add at the bottom of the Today, Calendar or Records tab and choose Photos & screenshots or File (PDF). The app only receives the photos you pick in the photo picker.
  2. Pick who's in it — Under People in this item, choose the family members the notice is about. A notice about a child can go to AI only after a guardian's consent is confirmed.
  3. Organize with AI — Each notice photo or PDF page uses 1 AI use. It's usually done within 15 seconds, and the result also shows up in your Inbox.
  4. Review suggestions — The dates and to-dos the AI found appear as suggestions. Compare dates and times with the original and fix anything wrong. You can ask a family member to take a reply or a supplies list right on this screen.
  5. Save — Nothing is saved until you check it. Until then only you can see it; when you save, you choose whether to share it with the family.

The first time you open the app, See a sample notice walks through this with a sample newsletter:

"Grade 3 field trip on Oct 17. Please arrive by 8:40 AM. Bring: lunch box, water, picnic mat. Please hand in the sign-up form to the homeroom teacher by next Tuesday."

The sample screen shows "Grade 3 field trip (Oct 17 · 8:40 AM)" and "Pack field trip supplies (lunch box, water, picnic mat)" as items to check, and "Hand in the sign-up form" as an item that needs a date, quoting "by next Tuesday" from the original. The sample is never saved.

Weekly classes as repeating events

A class at the same time every week doesn't need a photo each time. Add it once as a repeating event.

  1. Add event — Enter a title (say, Seoa's swim class), the day and the time.
  2. Repeat — Choose every week and an end date. For a school term, use the last day of term; if you can't say, choose no end.
  3. With — Add the child. If no adult is going along, the Sunday heads-up lists it as "No one to go along", and you can tap I'll do it right there.
  4. Change one day only — For a make-up class or a closure, edit just that day. When you edit a repeating event, the app asks whether to change this event only or the whole series.

Got a text about a time change? Take a screenshot and use Add. Check the date before saving here too.

Replies and supplies as to-dos, with an owner

What slips through most often isn't the event date but the reply deadline and things to bring. Make them to-dos rather than events, and give each one an owner.

  • Supplies as a checklist. Inside a "Pack field trip supplies" to-do, add lunch box, water and picnic mat as checklist items and tick them off as you pack.
  • Replies with a due date. Give "Hand in the sign-up form" a due date, and you'll get a reminder as it gets close.
  • One owner. When you ask someone to take a to-do, they answer with I'll do it or Can't do it. Anything nobody took is collected under No one assigned in the Sunday heads-up.

Before you use AI to organize notices

  • AI can be wrong. Everything it makes is labeled "AI-generated". Always check relative dates such as "next Tuesday" and times against the original.
  • Each adult decides. AI turns on only for adults who give their own consent. Without AI, Type it in creates the same events and to-dos.
  • There are limits. The free plan has 5 AI uses a month, shared by the family and refilled on the 1st. There are daily limits too (20 per person, 30 per family).
  • Only a small copy is sent. The phone shrinks the photo and removes its location before sending. The original stays on your phone.
  • Children's information gets extra care. A notice about a child is read by AI only with a guardian's consent. For items without a child, you confirm there is no child's name, face or contact info before sending.

FAQ

Does it connect to my school's app?

No. AXFamily isn't made by a school and doesn't connect to school apps. Take a screenshot of the message and use Add.

How many AI uses does one notice photo take?

One photo is 1 use, and each PDF page is 1 use. If AI fails to answer, the uses are given back (up to 4 a month).

Can I organize notices without AI?

Yes. Choose Type it in under Add, or add events and to-dos yourself. Events, to-dos and reminders all work without AI.

Does my whole family see the notices I add?

They start private. Share them with the family when you save, or change who can see them later to pick specific people.
