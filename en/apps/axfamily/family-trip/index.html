Plan a trip around naps, then keep photos together

With kids, the hard part of a trip is the timetable more than the place. Here is one flow for a plan that fits naps and meals, a packing list that remembers what you forgot, and photos sorted afterwards.

By SugarMount · 5 min read · Last updated November 22, 2026

This guide is based on AXFamily 1.2.0, an Android app by SugarMount. Every family, place and event in the text and screens is made up. The trip planner and family memories are AI features, so each adult who wants them turns on AI with their own consent.

Start with your family's daily rhythm

Naps and meals are the first things a trip schedule breaks. In AXFamily, write down each person's daily rhythm, and trip and weekend plans put rest and meal times around it.

  • Where — On the Family tab, open a person to find their daily rhythm: wake up, nap, lunch, dinner and bedtime. Anything left empty uses a general guide.
  • Who sees it — Daily rhythms are visible to the whole family.
  • Kids' routines don't go to AI — The app itself uses a child's nap and bedtime to block out rest time. They are never sent to the AI model.

Plan the trip

  1. Plan a trip — Tap Plan a trip on the Today tab or in Ask the family. Pick where, when and who's going, then tap Make plan. A trip can be up to 5 days.
  2. Read it day by day — The plan has Schedule, Packing, Budget and Roles tabs. The schedule includes rest and meal times from your daily rhythm, and each place comes with Find in map app and an alternative.
  3. Check what's marked — Opening hours, prices and travel times, which AI can't know for sure, are marked Needs checking. Confirm them from each place's official information before you go.
  4. Every adult on the trip accepts — A plan is added to the calendar, to-dos and the trip card only after every participating adult accepts. If anyone declines, nothing changes, and you can adjust it and make it again.

A plan only uses records every participating adult can see. At first only the participating adults see the plan; with Show to the whole family on, the whole family can, once the other adults turn it on too.

Packing: what you forgot last time comes first

The Packing tab splits kids' and adults' things and shows why each item is there (age group, routine, general suggestion and so on). The most useful part is your last trip.

  1. Mark items after the trip — When you're back, mark each item Used, Forgot or Not needed.
  2. See it next time — In the next trip plan, items you marked Forgot come first, tagged Forgotten last trip.
  3. See who packs what — Each packing group shows who has it, or that no one is assigned yet. The Roles tab suggests how to split the jobs.

The Budget tab works the same way: amounts come from your last trip, what you type in, then AI estimates, and estimates are marked for checking. The app adds up the total.

Lighter plans for the weekend

For a weekend close to home, use Plan the weekend. It gives two options for each free slot, fitted to your family's free time and daily rhythm. Pick one per slot and accept.

After the trip: group events and photos into an album

Trip photos end up scattered across everyone's phones. AXFamily's family memories groups a trip's events and photos so the family can look back together.

  1. Add photos — On the Photos tab, make a New album or tap Add photos in one. Only a smaller copy with the location removed is uploaded; the original stays on your phone.
  2. Check AI suggestions — Once a week the app looks through family records and suggests trip and photo groupings, such as "Make an album 'Autumn camping' from 9 photos" or "Add 3 events or tasks to a trip". Open AI suggestions on the Family tab; nothing changes until you tap Review and save, and only you see the suggestions.
  3. Open the trip card — The trip's events, to-dos and album come together on one trip card. From the album, Open trip card takes you back to that trip's plans.
  4. Ask later — In Ask the family, ask "When was our beach trip last year?". It answers from your records and shows which one it used, and says so when nothing matches.

Family memories use only records from adults who agreed to AI. Names and notes of children under 14 are never sent to AI, and album suggestions don't count toward AI uses.

What it can't do

  • It isn't a place-recommendation service. A plan is a draft fitted to your family's schedule and rhythm. Check opening hours, prices and bookings yourself.
  • It doesn't book or pay. Book stays and restaurants with each service directly.
  • Photo storage has a limit. 1,000 photos on the free plan and 10,000 with Family Plus. They are small copies for the family to look at, not full-quality backups.
  • AI can be wrong. Plans and suggestions are labeled "AI-generated", and you can report odd results in the app.

FAQ

How long a trip can it plan?

Up to 5 days at a time. A weekend plan uses 2 AI uses, a 1 to 3 day trip 3, and a 4 or 5 day trip 4.

What if another adult declines the plan?

If anyone declines, nothing goes into the calendar, to-dos or trip card. You can adjust the plan and make it again.

Is my child's nap time sent to AI?

No. The app uses a child's daily rhythm itself to set rest time, and doesn't send it to AI.

Are original photos uploaded?

No. The phone uploads a smaller copy with the location removed. The original stays on your phone.
