Share a family calendar and decide who does what

Putting every event in one place is only half of it. When each to-do also shows who is on it, nobody has to ask twice. Here is how to set that up with AXFamily, a shared family calendar for Android.

By SugarMount · 7 min read · Last updated November 22, 2026

This guide is based on AXFamily 1.2.0, an Android app by SugarMount. Every name and event in the text and screens is made up.

What goes wrong when plans live in a group chat

A family group chat is quick, but it is a poor place to keep a schedule. New messages push last week's class timetable out of sight, and "who said they'd do that?" comes up again.

  • Nobody's name stays on the task. "I'll handle it" gets buried in the thread and never reaches a calendar.
  • Clashes are easy to miss. When a child's swim class and an adult's meeting sit in two different calendars, no one notices they overlap.
  • Shared and private plans get mixed. You can't post a surprise gift run in the family chat.

So a family calendar that lasts settles three things: where plans go, who can see them, and who takes care of them. The steps below go through them in that order.

Step 1: Create your family and invite people

  1. Create a family — Sign up with a Google account or email the first time you open the app, and give your family a name. Accounts are for adults 19 or older.
  2. Invite adults — On the Family tab, tap Invite family → Send invite message. The app writes a message with an invite code, and your phone's share sheet sends it to your partner or the kids' grandparents.
  3. Accept the invite — The other person installs the app, taps I was invited and pastes the message. Each code works once and expires, so send it only to the person you are inviting.
  4. Add kids as profiles — Kids don't sign up. Add them as a profile with a name and birthday; they don't need a phone. Birthday reminders are set here too.

The person who created the family (the owner) or an admin can invite. The simplest start: one adult creates the family, then invites the other adults who look after the kids, one at a time.

Step 2: Choose who sees what

AXFamily is a shared calendar, but new events and records start private. You widen only the plans your family needs to see. Open an event and tap its sharing setting to get the Who can see sheet.

SettingWho sees itGood for
Only meOnly you. Not even the family admin.Buying a surprise gift, your own doctor's appointment
Selected peopleThe family members you pick.A meeting with a child's tutor that two adults need to know about
Whole familyEveryone in the family now and anyone who joins later.School runs, family events, trips

With Time only, your family sees that you are busy at that time, but not the title or details. It works well for work meetings you don't want to share but still need to block out.

Items you keep private never show up when someone else asks the AI assistant. A family admin can't open another adult's private items either.

Step 3: Decide who does what with "I'll do it"

Once plans are in one place, decide who handles each one. In AXFamily every to-do keeps the name of the person who took it.

  1. Add a task — Tap Add task and enter a title and a due date. For a list of things to bring, add them as a Checklist (up to 50 items).
  2. Ask someone — To hand it to another family member, ask them to do it. A card such as "Dogyeom Yoon asked you to take this" appears on their Today tab.
  3. "I'll do it" or "Can't do it" — When they tap I'll do it, the family sees they are on it. If they can't this time, they tap Can't do it, and you see the reply so you can ask someone else.
  4. Check things off — Tick checklist items as they are done (after the task is taken). With notifications on, you get a reminder before the due date.

You don't have to ask first. On a task the whole family can see, anyone can tap I'll do it on something nobody has taken yet.

Sharing school runs and class pick-ups with grandparents

If grandparents help with drop-offs and pick-ups, the key is deciding which adult goes along to each child's event. A made-up week might look like this:

Child's eventRepeatGoes along
Seoa's swim class (Wed 4:30 PM)Every weekGrandma
Jiho art class (Tue, Thu 3:00 PM)Every weekDogyeom
Seoa piano (Fri 5:00 PM)Every weekNot decided yet
  1. Make kids' classes repeat — When you add the event, set Repeat to every week and choose an end date or a number of times. Add only the child under With.
  2. Pick who goes along — If an event with only a child on it has no adult going along, the Sunday heads-up and the Today tab show it as "No one to go along". Tap I'll do it on that line and a to-do such as "Go with: Seoa piano" is created in your name.
  3. See clashes early — If a child's event overlaps another event of the adult going along, it shows up under Overlapping plans, like Seoa's swim class and a team meeting in the screen below.

If grandparents find a new app hard, start by just looking at the Sunday heads-up together. They only need to tap the things they can take.

Check the week together on Sunday evening

A shared calendar lasts when the family looks at it together once a week. With notifications on, AXFamily gathers the coming week every Sunday evening:

  • No one assigned — to-dos nobody took, and children's events with no adult going along
  • Overlapping plans — a child's event that clashes with the companion's other plans
  • Due — to-dos due this week and who has them
  • Birthdays — family birthdays within 7 days

It arrives at 8 PM by default, and you can change the time in settings. The list is made on your phone, and the notification shows counts only, never titles or names. The Sunday heads-up is free and doesn't count toward AI uses.

Small rules that keep a family calendar going

  • One person per to-do. "Either of us" often ends up as neither. Even for shared jobs, one person keeps track.
  • Family plans for the family, yours for you. Don't share everything on day one; widen only what others need. Work meetings can be "Time only".
  • Weekly things repeat. Classes and practice that happen every week go in once as a repeating event.
  • Five minutes on Sunday. When the heads-up arrives, sort out the unassigned items first and talk about any clash that day.

What it can't do

  • It's an Android app. It runs on Android 12L or later and needs the internet, since your family shares records.
  • It doesn't sync with other calendar apps. Add events from another calendar yourself, or take a screenshot and use Add to organize it.
  • Notifications must be allowed on the phone. If the app's notifications are off in phone settings, event reminders and the Sunday heads-up won't arrive.
  • None of this needs AI. Invites, sharing settings, "I'll do it", repeating events and the Sunday heads-up all work without AI.

FAQ

Is the shared family calendar free?

Events, to-dos, reminders, the Sunday heads-up and sharing settings are free, with one banner ad at the bottom of the screen. The Family Plus subscription removes ads for the whole family and adds more AI uses.

Can my partner see all my events?

No. New events start private. Only events you switch to the whole family or to selected people are visible. Not even the family admin can see your private events.

Do kids need an account?

No. Kids are added as profiles without signing up, and they don't need a phone.

What happens when grandma taps "Can't do it"?

The task shows "{name} replied they can't do it" to the person who asked. They can tap New task with same details to ask someone else, or take care of it themselves.

Can the weekly heads-up come on another day?

The day is fixed to Sunday; only the time can change. During the week, the Today tab's Needs attention shows to-dos due soon with no one assigned, overlapping plans and children's events with no one going along.
