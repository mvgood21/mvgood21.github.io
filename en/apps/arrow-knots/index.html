Arrow Knots

Tap to untangle knotted arrows. 1,000 levels, bonus mini games and a new daily puzzle every day. Plays offline, no sign-up.

Get it on Google PlayPrivacy policy

How it plays

The arrows are tied in knots. Tap one and it slides out along its own body in the direction it points. If another arrow is in the way, it bumps, bounces back and you lose a heart. Clear the blockers first, empty the board, and you win.

  1. Tap an arrow with a clear path — find an arrow whose way ahead is open and tap it to send it off the board.
  2. Work out the order — if an arrow is blocked, remove whatever is in its way first. Bumping into another arrow costs a heart.
  3. Empty the board — clear every arrow to finish. Finish inside the target time with hardly any slips for 3 stars.

What's inside

1,000 levels in four parts
In Part 1 (levels 1–200) every board fits on one screen and the order gets trickier. After level 1,000, new boards keep coming.
A bonus mini game every 25 levels
Each world ends with one of four: Neon Rush (60 seconds of quick clears), Shadow Memory (remember where the arrows were), Number Order (clear them in the order shown) and Boss Time Attack (a big board against the clock). Your stars decide the coins in the reward chest.
Daily puzzle
A new board every day, the same for everyone; Monday is the gentlest, the weekend the toughest. Solve it on the day to grow your streak, and reach 7, 15 or 25 days in a month for a bronze, silver or gold trophy.
Coins and hard levels
First clears earn coins by stars, and 100 coins buy a hint. Hard levels pay double.
Zoom on big boards
From level 201 the boards outgrow the screen. Tap to zoom in, drag to look around.
Hints
3 per level (4 on a few of the biggest boards). A hint lights up the arrow that opens up the most of the board and pulls it out for you. Your combo survives.
Hearts and continues
3 to 5 hearts, depending on how many arrows there are. Clear arrows in a row without a miss and a lost heart comes back. Out of hearts? Watch an ad to continue with 2 hearts, once per try.
Combos and target time
Clear arrows within about a second of each other to build a combo. Every level has a target time, but going over doesn't fail you. You can hide the timer from the pause menu.
Three-board tutorial
Covers tapping, getting blocked and finding the order. No hearts are lost while you learn.
Easy colors, plays offline
The six arrow colors were checked in red-green and blue-yellow color blindness simulations. Plays without an internet connection.

Ads and privacy

  • Ads are served by Google AdMob and its partner Liftoff, which may use device information such as your advertising ID.
  • From level 4 there's a banner at the bottom of the screen. The tutorial and levels 1–3 have none.
  • Full-screen ads show up now and then, only between levels after a win. There are none until you've cleared 15 levels, and never after a loss or in the middle of a level.
  • If you leave the app and come back much later, you may see one ad.
  • Ads for a hint, a continue, double coins or keeping a streak only play when you choose them. The bonus offer between levels can be skipped.
  • A one-time purchase removes the banner, full-screen and comeback ads, and adds 10 hints. Ads for a hint or a continue are still there whenever you want them. A pack of 15 hints is also available.
  • No account, no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your device; we don't run a server.
  • Where consent is required, you can change your choice anytime under Privacy settings in the pause menu.

For details, see the Arrow Knots privacy policy.
