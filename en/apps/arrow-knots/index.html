Arrow Knots Coming soon

Tap to untangle knotted arrows. 300 levels, works offline, no sign-up.

Arrow Knots: Arrow Puzzle (package name com.sugarmount.arrows) is being prepared for release on Google Play. The install link will be posted on this page once it is available.

How it plays

The arrows are tied in knots. Tap one and it slides out along its own body in the direction it points. If another arrow is in the way, it bumps, bounces back and you lose a heart. Clear the blockers first, empty the board, and you win.

  1. Tap an arrow with a clear path — find an arrow whose way ahead is open and tap it to send it off the board.
  2. Work out the order — if an arrow is blocked, remove whatever is in its way first. Bumping into another arrow costs a heart.
  3. Empty the board — clear every arrow to finish. No mistakes and inside the target time earns 3 stars.

What's inside

300 levels
From small boards to dense heart, circle, cat, trophy and anchor shapes.
3-step tutorial
Walks you through tapping, getting blocked and finding the order. No hearts are lost while you learn.
Combos
Remove arrows within a second of each other and the combo climbs, with a flash at x5, x10 and x20.
Target time
Every level has one, but missing it never fails the level. The timer can be hidden or turned off in Settings.
Hints
3 per level. A hint shows an arrow you can remove right now.
Zoom and settings
Pinch to zoom on big boards, and switch sound and vibration on or off in Settings.

Offline and privacy

  • No ads.
  • The app has no internet permission, so it plays fully offline. The only permission it requests is vibration.
  • No account. Start playing without signing in.
  • No data is collected, including usage analytics and crash reports. Progress (level, best times and scores, settings) is stored only on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

For details, see the Arrow Knots privacy policy.
