SugarCard

SaengSaeng Card

For Lunar New Year, Chuseok, Christmas, birthdays and other days that matter, pick a greeting and a card, edit the message and send it straight to KakaoTalk or text messages. The app is in Korean.

Get it on Google PlayPrivacy policy

What it does

  • Greetings for every occasion

    Holidays, birthdays, weddings, seasonal greetings, comfort and thanks.

  • Your own words

    Edit the prepared message or add the recipient's name.

  • Share in one tap

    Send to KakaoTalk, text messages or social apps with the share button.

  • Message history

    Messages you sent are kept on your device, and you can delete them.

Privacy

  • Messages and finished cards are made and stored on your device. When you share, they go only to the app you pick.
  • The app includes ads (Google AdMob) and usage statistics (Google Analytics).
  • When the main screen loads the Naver search page, your IP address, browser information (User-Agent) and cookies may reach Naver. Your messages are not included.
  • No account is created.

See the SugarCard privacy policy for details.

Frequently asked questions

What occasions does it cover?

Lunar New Year, Chuseok, Christmas, birthdays, weddings, seasonal greetings, comfort and thanks.

Can I change the message?

Yes. Edit the prepared greeting or add the recipient's name.
