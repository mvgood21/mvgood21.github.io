SugarCard

SugarCard is a Korean-language Android app for sending Lunar New Year, Chuseok and Christmas greeting cards with your own message written on the card picture.

Google Play SaengSaeng Card

Get it on Google PlaySugarCard privacy policy

Use it to

  • Send a Christmas greeting card on KakaoTalk
  • Make a Lunar New Year greeting card
  • Send a Chuseok greeting card

What it does

  • Cards for each holiday

    Lunar New Year, Chuseok and Christmas each come with four card styles. The holiday that fits the current month shows first.

  • Your own greeting

    Type a greeting and it is placed on the card picture.

  • Share in one tap

    Send the card image to KakaoTalk, text messages or another app you pick. Christmas cards can also go out as a snow video.

  • Greeting history

    Greetings you sent are kept on your device, and you can delete them.

Data and permissions

  • Messages and finished cards are made and stored on your device. When you share, they go only to the app you pick.
  • The app includes ads (Google AdMob), usage statistics (Google Analytics), and remote config and notifications (Firebase).
  • When the main screen opens and the app connects to the Naver search page, your IP address, browser information (User-Agent) and cookies may reach Naver. Your messages are not included.
  • No account is created.

See SugarCard privacy policy for details.

What it can't do

  • Cards cover three holidays only: Lunar New Year, Chuseok and Christmas. There are no birthday or wedding cards.
  • Sharing as a video works for Christmas cards only. Other cards are shared as images.
  • The app is in Korean only.

Frequently asked questions

What occasions does it cover?

Lunar New Year, Chuseok and Christmas. There are no birthday or wedding cards yet.

Can I write my own greeting?

Yes. The greeting you type is placed on the card picture.
