CarDrawer · Pilot

We're looking for 5 pilot dealerships

Use it on real deals for 4 weeks while we measure the time it saves together. If it saves none, you don't pay.

By SugarMount · 2 min read · Last updated October 19, 2026

Who it's for

  • Shops of 2 to 10 people that buy and resell their own stock
  • Buying through two or more channels (platform auctions, auction houses, direct)
  • Matching receipts and papers in chat rooms or spreadsheets today
  • We visit Suwon and Gangseo (Seoul) complexes first; other areas welcome

Terms

  • First 4 weeks free, then the Team rate of ₩149,000 a month (excl. VAT)
  • A 12-month founding-customer price lock instead of a discount
  • We set up members, cost items and deadline rules for you
  • Bank transfer against a tax invoice; we ask for consent again before billing

How it runs

  1. Apply with your shop details and how to reach you.
  2. 15-minute demo: with masked copies from one recently closed deal, we build a per-car cost sheet and a missing-papers list.
  3. Contract and setup: sign the pilot agreement and the data processing agreement, install the app and set it up together.
  4. Four weeks of use: we check in weekly, fix what blocks you and log the time spent.
  5. Decision: look at the time saved together and decide whether to continue.

Application form

The form is coming soon

For now, please apply by email with your shop name, area (complex), team size and how to reach you. We reply as soon as we can.

Apply by email

Please don't send ID numbers, customer details or passwords.

Personal information notice

Items(Required) shop name, your name, contact (mobile or email), area · (Optional) team size, monthly purchases, buying channels, current tools, message
PurposeConfirming applications, scheduling demos, selecting pilot shops
Retention6 months after pilot selection ends. If you sign, the data moves to contract records under our terms and privacy policy
Your right to refuseYou may refuse; we then cannot accept the application. Email applications are handled the same way
Processor and transferThe form is stored in Google Forms (Google LLC, USA and other countries)

Product news (marketing) goes only to people who opt in separately; you can apply without it. We never cold-contact public dealership numbers with marketing.
