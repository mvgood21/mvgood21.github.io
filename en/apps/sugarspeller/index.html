SugarSpeller

Hangul Checker

Paste text, share it from another app or pick it from the text selection menu, and SugarSpeller fixes Korean spelling and word spacing in one pass. Up to 3,000 characters at a time for free, and up to 7,000 with a subscription.

Get it on Google PlayPrivacy policy

What it does

  • Check by sharing

    Share from KakaoTalk or a notes app, or select text and pick SugarSpeller from the menu. No copy and paste needed.

  • Long text in one go

    Free 3,000 characters, Basic 4,000, Premium 7,000 per check.

  • Choose the checker

    Use the Naver or Daum (Kakao) spelling checker. If one fails, the app retries once with the other.

  • Why is it wrong?

    Tap a correction to get a short AI explanation. Only when you tap, the corrected word and error type are sent; surrounding sentences are not.

  • History

    Originals and corrections are kept on your device so you can look back.

  • Daily Korean quiz

    More than 100 tricky spelling questions, each with an explanation.

Privacy

  • Text you check is sent only to the Naver or Daum (Kakao) spelling checker. It is not sent to our servers.
  • Your check history stays on your device.
  • Only when you tap “Why is it wrong?”, the corrected word and error type go through our server (Google Firebase) to Google Vertex AI (Gemini). Surrounding sentences are not sent.
  • The app includes ads (Google AdMob with mediation partner Liftoff), usage statistics (Google Analytics) and remote config and notifications (Firebase).
  • To count daily AI explanations, the app creates an anonymous identifier (Firebase anonymous account). It is not linked to a name, email or phone number.

See the SugarSpeller privacy policy for details.

Frequently asked questions

How many characters can I check at once?

3,000 for free, 4,000 with Basic and 7,000 with Premium.

Is my text stored or uploaded?

Text is sent only to the Naver or Daum spelling checker to be checked, not to our servers. Your history stays on your device. When you tap “Why is it wrong?”, the corrected word and its explanation may be cached on our server (Google Firebase).

Can I check text from other apps?

Yes. Pick SugarSpeller from another app's share menu, or from the menu that appears when you select text.
