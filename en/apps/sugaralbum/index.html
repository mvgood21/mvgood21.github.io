SugarAlbum

Easy Video Maker

Pick photos and SugarAlbum turns them into a video. Check the result in the preview, then choose a title and resolution and save it. Photos and videos are processed on your device only.

Get it on Google PlayPrivacy policy

What it does

  • Just pick photos

    Pick photos in order and the scenes are arranged for you.

  • Preview

    See the finished video before you save.

  • Title and resolution

    Add a title and choose a resolution such as FHD (1920×1080).

  • Background music

    Uses music bundled with the app.

Privacy

  • Photos, videos and the videos you make are processed on your device and never uploaded.
  • Photo analysis for arranging scenes (date taken, quality and, on some devices, face positions) also happens on the device.
  • The app includes ads (Google AdMob) and usage statistics (Google Analytics).

See the SugarAlbum privacy policy for details.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos and videos are processed on your device only.

What resolutions can I save?

The resolution you choose on screen, such as FHD (1920×1080).
