SugarClip

SugarClip is an Android app that sends the text you copy to the note app you chose and keeps a searchable history of your clips on your device.

Google Play

Get it on Google PlaySugarClip privacy policy

Use it to

  • Find something you copied earlier
  • Collect copied text in a note app automatically
  • Keep content copied from a web page with its formatting

What it does

  • Share by copying

    No pasting: copy and the share screen of your chosen app opens. On Android 10 and later this usually works while SugarClip is open.

  • Pick the target app

    KakaoTalk “Chat with myself”, Google Keep, OneNote or Evernote.

  • History and search

    Copied text and web page HTML are saved with the time and source label, and you can search them.

  • Pause and resume

    Pause the service when you don't need auto-sharing.

Data and permissions

  • Copied content (clips) stays on your device and is not sent to our servers or to ad and analytics services.
  • Shared clips go only to the share screen of the app you pick.
  • The app includes ads (Google AdMob), usage statistics (Google Analytics), and remote config and notifications (Firebase).

See SugarClip privacy policy for details.

What it can't do

  • On Android 10 and later, system limits mean it usually records only what you copy while SugarClip is open. Nothing is recorded in the background.
  • Sharing targets are limited to KakaoTalk Chat with myself, Google Keep, OneNote and Evernote.

Frequently asked questions

Does copied text leave my phone?

No. Clips are stored on your device and go to another app only when you share them.

Which apps can I share to?

KakaoTalk “Chat with myself”, Google Keep, OneNote or Evernote.
