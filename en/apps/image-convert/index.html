Convert Image JPG PNG WEBP

An Android app that turns HEIC, PNG and WebP photos into JPG for forms, transparent PNG or small WebP, saved as new files. It converts whole batches on your device without uploading anything.

Use it to

  • Convert HEIC photos from a Galaxy phone to JPG
  • Convert PNG to JPG with a white background
  • Convert WebP images to JPG
  • Save a logo as a transparent PNG

What it does

Use it when a form only accepts JPG, a logo has to be delivered as a transparent PNG, or shop photos need to become small WebP files. Your original photos stay as they are; results are saved as new files.

  1. Pick photos — use the Android photo picker or “Pick from files”. Photos picked from files keep their original name with the new extension. Tap a photo if it should be saved in a format of its own.
  2. Choose the format and options — only the options for the chosen format appear: quality and the colour behind transparent areas for JPEG, whether to keep transparency for PNG, lossy or lossless plus quality for WebP. The long edge, black & white and file naming are set here too, with a preview.
  3. Check, then save or share — compare the original and the result side by side, then save or share. Saved photos go to the Pictures/SugarConvert folder as new files.

WebP for the web

Transparent PNG
Keeps the transparency of logos and graphics.
Black & white JPG
Removes colour for documents and print.

Profiles and run history

Save a format, quality, size and naming rule under a name of your choice and reapply it from the home screen in one tap. Up to 8 profiles can be saved. The recent run history (date, counts, format, sizes) stays on the device and never records file names.

One failed photo never stops the rest, and you can retry only the failed ones. The result screen shows the real file size before and after, and says so when a file got larger.

Data and permissions

  • Your photos, file names and results are never sent to our servers or anywhere else. We do not run a server.
  • Converting photos needs no internet connection. Ads and the Pro purchase work only while you are online.
  • No storage permission is requested. The app can read only the photos you pick yourself in the photo picker or file picker.
  • No account is created. Profiles, run history and settings stay on the device and are deleted with the app.
  • In the free version, ads are served by Google AdMob, and the ads SDK may collect data such as the advertising ID and IP address under Google’s policies. Where required, a consent form is shown; you can change your choice under “Ad privacy options” in Settings. Pro shows no ads.
  • Anonymous usage stats (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are on by default and can each be switched off in Settings. Neither includes your photos or file names.

See the Convert Image privacy policy for details.

What it cannot do

  • GIF and BMP files and PDFs are not converted; they are left out when you pick. Animated images such as animated WebP are not converted either, and fail on their own.
  • No cropping, rotating or retouching. Size can only be reduced by the long edge, and photos are never enlarged.
  • Saving as PNG or lossless WebP does not restore detail a JPEG source has already lost, and the file can get larger.

Questions

Can I convert HEIC photos from an iPhone to JPG?

Yes, when your device can decode HEIC, the app converts it to JPG. The “JPG for forms” preset saves it at quality 90. A HEIC photo the device cannot read fails on its own, and the rest of the batch continues.

What are converted files named?

Photos picked with “Pick from files” keep their original name with the new extension. The Android photo picker does not tell apps the file name, so photos picked there are saved as SugarConvert plus the save time.

What happens to the transparent background when a PNG becomes a JPG?

JPG cannot store transparency, so transparent areas are filled with the background colour you pick: white by default, or black or gray. To keep transparency, save as PNG or WebP.

Are my photos uploaded?

No, photos are never uploaded and are converted on your device only. See Data and permissions.
