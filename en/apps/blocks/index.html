Block Turn

A stuck board and just three pieces. Which order, and which spot, clears the lines in one go? A new daily puzzle, no timers. Plays offline, no sign-in.

Get it on Google PlayPrivacy policy

How it plays

You get a half-filled board and three set pieces. Place all three and clear the target number of lines (say, 4) to win. The pieces are fixed, so it's about finding the right order and spots, not luck.

  1. Check the board and the goal — the top of the screen shows the line goal and how many lines you've cleared so far.
  2. Drag the three pieces onto the board — lines that will clear light up before you drop. Pieces float above your finger so you can see where they go.
  3. Reach the goal to win — stuck? Start the puzzle over, as often as you like.

What's inside

300 three-move puzzles
A fixed board, three pieces and a line goal. They ramp up from guided first steps to hard turns.
No timers, no energy
Retry as often as you like. Stuck a few times? Step through the solution for free.
Today's Turn
Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. Share how you did with friends.
Classic score chase
Place pieces on an 8×8 board and clear rows and columns for points. Multi-line and back-to-back clears earn combo points, and you get one free undo per game.
Controls your way
Adjust how high a piece floats above your finger while dragging, or tap to place instead of dragging.
Two skins: Night sky and Daylight
Soft jelly blocks under a night sky, or a bright daylight look. Sound and vibration can be turned off.
No sign-in, plays offline
No account needed, and it works without an internet connection.

Ads and privacy

  • Ads are served by Google AdMob and its partner Liftoff, which may use device information such as your advertising ID.
  • No banner and no ads between games during the guide puzzles and your first three games.
  • Ads between games show only after a game ends, when you tap Next, Play again or Home on the result screen. Never while you are placing pieces.
  • These ads and the return ad wait at least 2 minutes after the previous full-screen ad.
  • If you leave the app for more than 4 minutes and come back, an ad may show once (up to twice a day), but not if you were in the middle of a game.
  • Hint and revive ads play only when you tap their button.
  • A one-time purchase removes the banner, ads between games and the return ad. Optional reward ads (hints, revives) stay available.
  • No account, no sign-up. Your progress and settings are saved on your device; we don't run a server.
  • Where consent is required, you can change your choice anytime under Privacy options in Settings.

For details, see the Block Turn privacy policy.
