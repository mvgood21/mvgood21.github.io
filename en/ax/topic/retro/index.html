Retrospectives and numbers

Looking back over a period of work: what went fast and what went wrong, with numbers we counted ourselves from the git history and QA reports.

4 notes

  • Working with agents
  • Testing and release
  • Android development
  • Game development
  • Play Store and monetization
  • Website and search

Notes

  • Daily users rose after the redesign while organic D1 retention fell from 15.2% to 11.2%

    After an August 2025 redesign, D1 retention of our spelling app's organic cohorts fell from 15.2% to 11.2%; 70% of lost daily users were 31-365 days old.

  • We built three photo apps before checking the market: this year's new apps had a median of 1,166 installs

    We scraped Google Play only after building three photo apps. The 32 compress apps from 2026 had a median of 1,166 installs; 11 apps were logged as on hold.

  • A year of AI coding in 701 commits: from "complete" to cause and verification

    701 commits recounted: after adopting AI, messages grew from 26 to 233 characters, but fixes stating a cause went from 1 of 29 to 56 of 73 only a year later.

  • How we shipped four Android apps in 20 days with an AI coding agent

    A solo developer and Claude Code put four Android apps on Google Play within 20 days of the first commit. 52 of 59 commits were co-authored with the agent.
