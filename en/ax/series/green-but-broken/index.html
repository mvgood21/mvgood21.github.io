When green does not mean tested

Six cases where an agent's 'tests pass' or a green CI checked nothing real, and how to test the tests themselves.

6 parts

Series contents

    October 19, 2026

    Our AI agent called a puzzle game crash-free, then it died on the first tutorial tap: a missing VIBRATE permission.

  2. Part 2What "100% E2E pass" really meant: deleting seven runners with zero expect() calls
