Reworking a long-running spelling app

Revisiting a long-running Korean spelling app with an agent: commit history, retention, the subscription funnel, update and rollout incidents, a removed feature and quiz reach.

8 parts

Series contents

  1. Part 1A year of AI coding in 701 commits: from "complete" to cause and verification

    October 24, 2026

    701 commits recounted: after adopting AI, messages grew from 26 to 233 characters, but fixes stating a cause went from 1 of 29 to 56 of 73 only a year later.

  2. Part 2Daily users rose after the redesign while organic D1 retention fell from 15.2% to 11.2%

    October 30, 2026

    After an August 2025 redesign, D1 retention of our spelling app's organic cohorts fell from 15.2% to 11.2%; 70% of lost daily users were 31-365 days old.

  3. Part 3A month of 'temporary error': our subscription details screen never waited for products, and 1 of 16 users started checkout

    October 29, 2026

    A Play review said the premium plan showed a 'temporary error' for a month.

  4. Part 4"6.7" < "6.11" is false: comparing Remote Config versions as strings froze content updates

    October 30, 2026

    A Korean spelling app compared Remote Config content versions as Kotlin strings, so from 6.10 on, devices stored at 6.2 to 6.9 got no quiz or notice updates.

  5. Part 5A splash that never moved on: addOnCompleteListener(this) and onStop

    October 31, 2026

    Our spelling app used addOnCompleteListener(this) for Remote Config; the listener was dropped at onStop, so a screen-off launch never left the splash.

  6. Part 6A 20% staged rollout went out at 100%: a completed release in the Play Publisher API cannot go back to staged

    October 21, 2026

    SugarSpeller 1.6.029 was meant for 20% via the Play Publisher API but was committed as completed; Play refused the move back to 20% with a 403.

  7. Part 710,001 lines of greeting cards: built with an AI agent, removed with one

    November 6, 2026

    We deleted 10,001 lines of AI-built greeting cards. With R8 in the same release, the APK fell from 25.66 to 13.15 MB; about 11 MB was card assets.

  8. Part 8Quiz reach rose from 2.6% to 17.7%, and 63% of quiz opens came from one morning notification

    November 9, 2026

    Quiz reach in a Korean spelling app rose from 2.6% to 17.7%, with 63% of opens from a morning local notification.
