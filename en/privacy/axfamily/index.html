Privacy policy

AXFamily

Effective: October 9, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app AXFamily (Google Play developer SugarMount), published by simpest (developer name SugarMount, “we”). It describes the build we currently distribute. Before we turn on a new feature or change how data is handled, we update this policy first. The Korean version prevails if the two differ.

1. Summary

  • AXFamily lets a family see and share events, to-dos, birthdays and material. Only adults aged 19 or older can create an account; children are added as profiles that never sign in.
  • The default sharing scope is Only me. Even the family admin cannot see another adult's private items.
  • We never send family data, children's data, content or screen context to the ad SDK (Google AdMob).
  • AI organizing sends material to Google Gemini (paid tier, not used for training) only when you ask. Every basic feature works with manual entry.
  • Photos are uploaded only as reduced copies with location and other metadata removed on the device. Originals never reach our servers.
  • You can delete your account and data in the app or on the deletion request page. Only payment records are kept for 5 years, as the law requires.

2. Purposes

  • Sign-up, sign-in and adult confirmation (self-declared 19 or older; the birth year is not stored)
  • Creating a household, invitations, member management and sharing by scope
  • Storing and syncing events, to-dos, birthdays, material, photos and comments within the family
  • On-device reminders and family activity push notifications (we send no advertising notifications)
  • AI organizing you request (suggested events and to-dos from your material)
  • Verifying, renewing and refunding the Family Plus subscription
  • Showing the banner ad on the free plan
  • Crash diagnosis (if you agree), blocking abuse and tampered apps, handling reports and support

4. Retention

  • Account, household, records and material: until you delete them or delete the account or household. We aim to delete operational data within 72 hours of a request.
  • Trash (deleted items): removed automatically after 30 days.
  • Private items of an adult who leaves a household: hidden from the family immediately and deleted after 30 days.
  • Deleting a household that has other adults: members are notified during a 7-day grace period, then it is deleted.
  • AI suggestions: unsaved suggestions are deleted after 7 days; AI processing records are kept 90 days after completion.
  • Reports: 180 days after submission.
  • Invitation links: expire after 24 hours by default (7 days at most).
  • Crash logs (Crashlytics): 90 days.
  • Payment records: 5 years under the Korean E-Commerce Act Enforcement Decree, Article 6 (pseudonymized, separate from family data, kept even after account deletion).
  • Backups: we currently keep no backups of operational data. If we turn backups on, we will first add their retention period here.

5. How we destroy data

When a retention period ends or you ask for deletion, our server functions delete the database documents and everything under them, and the reduced files in storage, so they cannot be read again. A file still used by other material is deleted when that material is deleted. Caches on devices are cleared on the next sync. Payment records kept by law are stored separately and deleted automatically when their period ends.

6. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell or provide personal data to third parties. The ad SDK (Google AdMob) does, however, process your advertising ID, IP address and similar data directly for Google's own advertising purposes. This is covered in 9-4. Ads and the overseas transfer table, and we ask for separate consent on the ad consent screen. We disclose data under a legal request only as far as the law requires.

7. Processors

We use these companies to run the service. Their data processing terms include security measures and limits on sub-processing.

  • Google LLC: authentication, database and server functions, push notifications, app integrity checks, crash reporting, AI organizing (Vertex AI Gemini), Google Play purchase verification
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: storing and serving reduced copies of photos and material

8. Overseas transfers

Adults' data is transferred as entrustment needed to perform the contract (Article 28-8(1)(3)(a)); ads rely on separate consent and children's data on guardian consent.

9-1. Children (under 14) and the rights of legal guardians

  • Children do not create accounts. A guardian (an account holder aged 19 or older) adds them as profiles.
  • When creating a child profile, the guardian must agree in the app to the processing and overseas transfer (table above) of the child's data before material about that child can be organized with AI. Material involving a child without consent can only be entered manually.
  • Guardians can withdraw consent at any time in settings. After withdrawal, material about that child is no longer sent to AI.
  • Guardians can view, correct and delete a child's data in the app or by contacting us below.

9-2. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop processing your personal data, and withdraw consent. Most of this can be done right in the app (Settings > Data and privacy > Data and connections). Account deletion can also be requested on the deletion request page. Requests by email are handled within 10 days after we confirm your identity, and we tell you the result. Items created by other members and other adults' private items belong to them, so we do not delete them at your request alone.

9-3. Automatic collection and how to refuse it

  • Push token and Firebase installation ID: used to deliver notifications. Removed when you turn off notifications in system settings or sign out.
  • App integrity check (App Check, Play Integrity): blocks tampered apps from our servers. It cannot be refused.
  • Crash reporting (Crashlytics): off by default and on only if you agree. You can turn it off in app settings.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad SDK. See Ads below for how to refuse.
  • The app uses no web cookies and includes no usage analytics SDK such as Google Analytics.

9-4. Ads

The free plan shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen (Google AdMob). The ad SDK sends your advertising ID, IP address, ad interactions, and device and diagnostic data directly to Google. We never put family data, children's data, event titles, content or screen context into ad requests. Before the first ad, we show an ad consent screen; if you refuse, you get non-personalized ads.

  • Change your choice in App settings > Data and privacy > Ad privacy options (shown where consent is required).
  • Delete the advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • With Family Plus, no one in the family sees ads.

9-5. AI organizing and sending data to a third-party AI

When you tap AI organizing, a reduced copy and the text of that material, plus family members' display names (excluding children without guardian consent), go through our server to Google Gemini (Vertex AI paid tier, Google Cloud Seoul region) to create suggested events and to-dos. Google does not use this data to train models. We ask for separate consent the first time you use AI organizing; without it, every basic feature still works with manual entry. AI suggestions are labeled as AI and become records only after you review and save them. You can report a wrong or inappropriate result in the app. We never use AI for birthdays, reminders, permissions, payments or usage decisions. AI organizing may be paused at times; while paused, nothing is sent to AI.

9-6. Other people's data in your material

School notices and screenshots may contain names and contact details of people outside the family, such as teachers or other parents. AI results do not store the names or contact details of people outside the family. They may remain in the uploaded reduced copy, so delete that material if needed. Anyone whose data appears in material can ask us to delete it using the contact below.

10. Security measures

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), and stored data uses the cloud providers' encryption at rest.
  • Database security rules limit access by household and sharing scope, and server functions accept only requests that pass app integrity checks.
  • Photo copies are separated per user and shown only through our server.
  • Operator access is kept to a minimum, and server logs never contain content, photos or inputs.
  • If personal data is leaked, or a leak is likely, we notify affected users within 72 hours.
