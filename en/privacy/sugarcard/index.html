SaengSaeng Card (SugarCard)

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app SaengSaeng Card – SugarCard (package name com.sugarmount.sugarcard), published by simpest under the developer name SugarMount (“we”, “us”). It describes, based on the actual implementation, what data the app handles and what it does not.

We do not operate a server for this app, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, contact details or address.

2. Card messages and images

  • Card images, fonts and background music are bundled with the app. Placing your message on a card and turning it into an image or video (MP4) all happens on your device.
  • The messages you type and the card images and videos you create are never sent to our servers or to ad or analytics services.
  • When you share a card, the app creates the image or video in its temporary storage (cache) and hands it to the app you choose (for example, a messenger). What happens next is governed by that app's policy.
  • When you tap share, the app saves the message, the time it was written and simple stats such as character count to an on-device history. You can delete entries one by one on the history screen, and the history is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • If you share text from another app into SaengSaeng Card, that text is only used to fill in the card message field.
  • The app does not access your photos, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

3. Ads

The app shows banner ads, app open ads, and native ads on the exit and history screens through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve ads and prevent abuse, the ad SDK may collect and process the following under Google's policies:

  • Device identifiers and the advertising ID
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate the device's approximate location)
  • Interactions with ads (impressions, clicks) and app usage information
  • Diagnostic information (ad SDK performance and errors)
  • Other items listed in the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

This data is collected and processed by Google; we do not access it directly. Please refer to Google's documentation for the exact items and purposes.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

Changing your ad choices

  • The current version does not show a separate ad consent screen or offer an ad settings menu inside the app.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings under Google > Ads. Once deleted, the advertising ID is no longer used for personalized ads.
  • On Android 13 and later, you can adjust ad-related features under Settings > Security & privacy > Ads privacy (menu names may vary by device).
  • A subscription reduces ads. The Basic plan removes app open ads and pop-up ads, and the Premium plan also removes banner ads. However, the current version shows the ad on the exit screen whether or not you subscribe.

4. Usage stats, app config and notifications

The app includes the Google Firebase SDK. Your messages, card images and videos are never included in any of the features below.

Usage stats (Firebase / Google Analytics)
May send automatically collected events such as app opens and screen usage, together with device information (model, OS version, app version, language and region settings, etc.), an app instance ID and the advertising ID, to Google. We use this to understand how the app is used and to improve it. The current version does not offer an in-app setting to switch this off.
App config (Firebase Remote Config)
When the app starts, it fetches notices, FAQs and maintenance or update information. Technical information such as the Firebase installation ID and app version is sent to Google in the process.
Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
Firebase creates a per-device notification token so that notifications can be delivered. We do not separately collect this token or link it to other information. On Android 13 and later, notifications are shown only if you allow them, and you can turn them off at any time in Android Settings.
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Firebase SDK data disclosure

5. Other internet connections

Because of code shared with an older spell-check feature, the app's main screen may connect to a Naver search page (search.naver.com) and download a web page when it opens. This request never includes the messages you type or your card contents. As with any web request, however, your IP address and device and connection details (such as the User-Agent) may be passed to Naver, and cookies set by Naver may be kept while the app is running and sent back. This is handled under the Naver Privacy Policy.

6. Subscription

  • Subscriptions for benefits such as fewer ads are handled through Google Play Billing. Payment details such as your payment method, name and billing address are handled by Google Play and are not shared with us.
  • The app only checks the subscription state provided by Google Play to decide whether to show ads and which benefits to apply. The subscription menu may not be shown in some versions.
  • Cancellations, refunds and purchase history follow Google Play's policies and procedures. Google Play Terms of Service

8. Accounts

The app does not create accounts or require sign-in. Because we hold no user accounts, there is no separate account deletion feature. Data the app stores on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.
