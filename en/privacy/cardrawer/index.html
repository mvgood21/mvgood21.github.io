This policy applies to CarDrawer (차서랍), a business service for used-car dealers in Korea, consisting of the Android app com.sugarmount.cardrawer and related web pages (the "Service"), provided by Simpest21 (Google Play developer name SugarMount; "we", "us"). It describes what the current build actually does. If we turn on a new feature or change how we process data, we update this policy first. If this English version differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

1. Summary

  • CarDrawer helps used-car dealerships and dealers organise receipts, documents, costs and deadlines per vehicle. It is a business service. Only people aged 19 or older who work in the used-car trade (owners, dealers, office staff) may create an account.
  • We handle two kinds of personal information:
    1. User data — information about the dealers and staff who create accounts (email, display name, etc.). We process it as the controller (개인정보처리자).
    2. Information inside a dealership's business records — names and contact details of vehicle owners, buyers and service providers that appear in contracts, registration certificates and receipts uploaded by a dealership. The dealership is the controller of this data. We process it only as the dealership's processor (수탁자) within the scope of our data processing agreement (Section 8).
  • As a rule we do not collect resident registration numbers, driver's licence numbers, passport numbers or alien registration numbers. The app asks you to mask them yourself with its masking tool before upload. Our server masks resident registration, mobile phone and card number patterns in the text the AI reads before it is stored. An original image you upload without masking may still contain such numbers (Section 3-3).
  • Photo metadata such as GPS location is removed on the device before upload. The app does not request location permission.
  • Document reading uses Google's generative AI (Vertex AI Gemini, paid tier). Google does not use this data to train its models. Processing may take place outside Korea (Section 9).
  • AI output is only a suggestion. Critical values such as amounts, vehicle assignment and accident-related marks are confirmed only after a person approves them. We make no automated decisions about individuals.
  • The app contains no advertising SDK (such as AdMob) and does not use the advertising ID. We do not sell personal information.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Settings > Delete account / organisation) or on our deletion request page.

3-3. What we do not process

  • Resident registration, driver's licence, passport and alien registration numbers are not collected as a rule. The app asks you to mask them yourself before upload. The app does not yet detect or mask these numbers automatically, so an original image uploaded without masking is stored as is and is also used for AI processing. Our server masks resident registration, mobile phone and card number patterns in the text the AI reads (extracted values, source wording, notes) before it is stored. If you find an unmasked original, delete it and upload a masked copy.
  • Location: no location permission. GPS and other photo metadata and PDF author fields are stripped on the device before upload. Addresses are handled only as text and are never geocoded.
  • No contacts, call logs, SMS, device calendar or advertising ID. No card numbers or other payment instruments.
  • We do not ask for sensitive information such as health or beliefs.

4-3. Deleting a staff account

When a staff member deletes their own account, the dealership's business records they uploaded are not deleted, because they belong to the dealership. Uploader and approver labels change to "deleted member" with an internal ID. Deletion of dealership records is requested by the dealership's administrator.

5. Destruction

Scheduled server jobs irreversibly delete database documents, sub-items and stored files (originals, AI input copies, exports), together with derived search fields and notification records. Device caches are cleared on next sync. Export download links expire after 15 minutes and export files are deleted after 7 days. We do not accept paper records; printed contracts are shredded.

6. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell personal information and do not provide user data to third parties, except: where required by law (e.g. a warrant); tax-invoice data sent to the National Tax Service (Hometax) under the VAT Act. Exports and shares you make yourself (CSV export, Android share sheet) are your actions; you choose the recipient. The app never posts, sends or submits on your behalf. Dealership records are processed as a processor and are not disclosed without the dealership's instruction.

8. Dealership records — when we act as processor

  1. The dealership is the controller for its customers' data; we are its processor under Article 26 of the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).
  2. We do not use this data outside the agreed tasks: not for our marketing, not for AI model training, not for other customers.
  3. Terms (tasks, sub-processors, safeguards, transfers abroad, return and deletion) are set in the Data Processing Agreement the dealership's administrator accepts in the app when creating the organisation (version and time stored). A signed paper version is available on request.
  4. Vehicle owners, buyers and other data subjects should first contact the dealership to access, correct or delete their data. If they contact us, we forward the request to the dealership without delay and assist.
  5. We provide dealerships with notice text they can use in their own privacy notices.
  6. When uploading, users confirm they are entitled to process the file. Screens and auction diagnoses from other platforms (e.g. HeyDealer, Encar) are stored only and never sent to AI.

10. AI notice (Korean AI Framework Act)

  • CarDrawer uses generative AI (Google Gemini) to read values from receipts, contracts and inspection reports and to draft sales descriptions from confirmed data. Before first use, the app shows this notice separately and records your acknowledgement.
  • AI output is labelled "AI suggestion" and becomes "Confirmed" (approver, time) only after a person approves it. Amounts, vehicle assignment, plate number, VIN, odometer, inspection and issue dates, accident or replacement marks, and seizure or mortgage status always require human approval.
  • AI output can be wrong. Drafts never include unverified claims such as "accident-free", "warranty" or "lowest price". The dealer is responsible for checking content before publishing it.
  • Use the "Report a problem" button next to any AI result to tell us about errors. Reports are used only for quality and error handling.
  • We do not use business records to train AI models, and under Vertex AI paid-tier terms Google does not either. We do not use free AI APIs.
  • Purchase-limit, deadline, cost and profit calculations use fixed formulas, not AI. We make no assessments or automated decisions about vehicle owners or buyers.

11. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing, and withdraw consent at any time — in the app (Settings), on the deletion request page, or by email to sugarmount21@gmail.com. We respond within 10 days after verifying your identity. We may restrict a request where the law requires retention or where it would infringe others' rights, and we will tell you why. Legal representatives and authorised agents may request with a power of attorney. Requests from a dealership's customers are handled first by the dealership (Section 8). People under 19 cannot create accounts, and we do not knowingly collect data of children under 14.

13. Marketing messages

We send marketing messages only to users who explicitly opted in, per channel (email, app push), default off. Messages between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. require separate consent. Each message is marked "(광고)" (advertisement) and shows the sender, contact and how to unsubscribe. You can withdraw in Settings > Notifications at any time; we confirm the result. We re-confirm consent every 2 years from the date it was given (Enforcement Decree of the Network Act, Art. 62-3). Service notifications (deadlines, approvals, security) are not marketing. Allowing Android notifications is not marketing consent. We never send unsolicited marketing to publicly listed dealership phone numbers or emails.

14. Security measures

  • Administrative: internal management plan, least-privilege operator access. Operators access dealership data only through a support grant approved by the dealership owner, with a stated purpose and expiry (default 24 hours), and it is logged.
  • Technical: TLS for all transfers, cloud encryption at rest, per-organisation isolation and role-based access (purchase prices, costs and counterparties behind separate permissions), app integrity checks on server functions, 15-minute signed download links, two-factor authentication for consoles, secrets in Secret Manager.
  • Logs: views, exports, permission changes and support access are audit-logged with IDs and masked values only. Server logs, crash reports and analytics never contain document text, extracted values or images.
  • Originals: uploads are never overwritten and are stored with a hash and upload details.
  • Incidents: if a breach or likely breach is confirmed, we notify affected users and authorities without delay (within 72 hours at the latest), and immediately notify the dealership where its records are involved.
