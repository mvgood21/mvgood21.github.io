SugarClip - Share Anything You Copy

2. Clipboard and copied content

  • SugarClip collects what you copy so you can reuse or share it. While the “clip saving service” is running, the app checks the clipboard about once per second and saves newly copied text as a clip. The service starts when you open the app and when the device restarts. While it runs, a status notification is shown, and you can pause or stop the service from its buttons. If you stop it, it starts again the next time you open the app. The app does not ask for the notification permission itself, so on Android 13 and later you may need to allow SugarClip notifications in Android Settings to see the status notification and its buttons.
  • On Android 10 and later, Android blocks background apps from reading the clipboard, so clips are usually saved only while the SugarClip screen is open. On Android 9 and earlier, copies may be saved even while the app is in the background.
  • For each clip the app stores the copied text (including its web/HTML form, if any), the time it was copied, the source label attached to the clipboard data, and the content type (text, web document, web address, etc.). This information is kept only in the app’s private storage on your device and is never sent to us or to the ad or analytics services. We do not operate a server for this app.
  • When a new clip is saved, the app opens the share screen of the app you selected under “Clip save” (KakaoTalk by default; you can choose Google Keep, OneNote or Evernote instead). You can also share a clip from its detail screen. Sharing uses Android’s share feature, and you decide in that app whether to send and to whom. What happens afterwards follows the policy of the app you chose.
  • Copied content may include sensitive information such as passwords, verification codes or account numbers. When copying such content, please pause or stop the service from the notification, and delete saved clips in the app. We cannot see your clips.
  • The app does not use Kakao Login or any other external account sign-in. The share target list is a list of package names of share target apps that we provide through Firebase Remote Config; the app does not collect or transmit the list of apps installed on your device.

3. Permissions

Reading the clipboard itself does not require an Android permission. The permissions the app declares and what they are used for:

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)Added by the Firebase Cloud Messaging library and used to show the clip saving service’s status notification and app notices. Other declarations (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, READ_LOGS, GET_CLIPS, READ_CLIPS, WRITE_CLIPS)Carried over from earlier versions. The current version does not request the “display over other apps” permission, and it does not read system logs (READ_LOGS is not granted to regular apps). None of these is used to collect personal data.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage (photos or files) or call log permissions, and it does not use an accessibility service.

4. Ads

The app shows banner ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve ads and prevent fraud, the ads SDK may collect and process the following information under Google’s policies. Clip content is never passed to the ads SDK.

  • Device identifiers and the advertising ID
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate the device’s approximate location)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, etc.) and app usage information
  • Diagnostics (performance and error information from the ads SDK)
  • Other items listed in Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

This data is collected and processed by Google; we do not access it directly. Please see Google’s documentation for the exact items and purposes.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

Your ad privacy choices

  • The current version has no in-app ad consent screen or ad settings menu.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads. Once deleted, it is not used for personalized ads.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in My Ad Center for your Google account.

5. Usage statistics, app configuration and push notifications (Firebase)

The app uses Google’s Firebase services. Your copied content (clips) is not included in any of them.

Usage statistics (Firebase / Google Analytics)
Automatically collected events such as app opens and screen views, device information (model, OS version, app version, language, approximate country/region, etc.), the app instance ID, the advertising ID and the install referrer are sent to Google. We use this to improve the service and understand usage. The current version has no in-app setting to turn this collection off.
App configuration (Firebase Remote Config)
When the app starts, it fetches the latest version information, notices and FAQ content, and the list of share target apps. In doing so, technical information such as the Firebase installation ID and app version is sent to Google. Fetched content is stored on your device.
Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
To allow notices to be delivered, a notification token is created for each device and registered with Google. We do not collect these tokens on any server of our own. If the app’s notifications are not allowed or are turned off in Android Settings, notifications are not shown.

6. Accounts and payments

The app does not create accounts or require sign-in. Because we keep no user accounts, there is no separate account deletion feature. The app has no in-app purchases.

9. Your rights

You (or a legal guardian, for children under 14) can do or request the following:

  • View and delete saved clips in the app
  • Pause or stop the clip saving service from its notification
  • Change the share target app under “Clip save”
  • Allow or block notifications at any time in Android Settings
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings
  • Send us a request to access, correct, delete or stop processing data sent to Google. We handle requests promptly to the extent we can verify, and for data that Google controls, we will point you to Google’s procedures.
