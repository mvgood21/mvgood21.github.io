Privacy policy

Arrow Knots

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy applies to version 1.0.0 of the Android game Arrow Knots: Arrow Puzzle (package name com.sugarmount.arrows), published by simpest under the developer name SugarMount (“we”, “us”). It describes, based on the actual implementation, what data the app handles and what it does not.

1. Summary

This game does not collect or share any personal data. It has no ads, no usage analytics, no crash reporting and no account, and it does not request the internet permission.

2. Permissions and internet access

  • The only Android permission the app requests is vibration (VIBRATE), used for haptic feedback during play, for example when an arrow is blocked. You can turn vibration off in Settings.
  • The app does not have the internet permission (INTERNET), so it cannot send any data to us or to third parties. We do not run any servers for the app.
  • The app does not request permissions such as location, contacts, camera, photos/storage or microphone.

3. Ads, analytics and third-party services

This version contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK and no in-app purchases. We do not provide or share data with third parties.

4. Accounts

The game does not create accounts or require sign-in. Because we hold no user accounts, there is no separate account deletion feature.

5. Retention and deletion

DataRetentionHow to delete
Game progress (level reached, best time, score and stars per level, tutorial completion) and settings (sound, vibration, timer and combo display)Stored only in the app's private storage on your device and never transmittedRemoved when you clear the app's data in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

We have no access to this data, so there is nothing held outside your device for us to show you or delete.

6. Children's privacy

This game is not directed at children under 13. The app does not collect personal data from any user, whatever their age.

7. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we update this page and its effective date. If a later version adds features such as ads or usage analytics, we will update this policy before that version is released, explaining what data is handled and how.
