1. Summary

  • The site has no sign-up, login or forms. We do not collect names or contact details through the site.
  • AX note pages may show Google AdSense ads, and Google may use cookies and similar technologies for them.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process access logs for security and operations.

2. Ads and cookies

2.1 Where ads appear

Ads appear only on AX note articles. The home page, note lists, series and topic pages, app pages and privacy policies carry no ads. Ads are labelled “Advertisements”.

2.2 Google AdSense

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and opt out of third-party vendors' cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

2.3 Visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Once ads are served, visitors from these regions are asked by a Google-certified consent tool before advertising cookies are used. You can still read every note if you decline. In that case you may see non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time with the settings link that consent screen leaves on the page.

2.4 Behavioral data

ItemDetails
What is collectedPages visited and when, ad views and clicks, cookie identifiers, browser and device information, IP address
HowAutomatically, by the ad network's script and cookies when you visit
Who collects itGoogle LLC (AdSense). We do not collect or receive it.
PurposeServing ads, interest-based ads, ad measurement, preventing invalid clicks
RetentionUnder each company's privacy policy
How to refuseThe settings in 2.2 and 2.3, and your browser settings in 2.5

We see only aggregate reports from the ad network, such as page views and ad earnings. They contain nothing that identifies you.

2.5 Blocking cookies

  • Chrome: Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies
  • Edge: Settings › Cookies and site permissions › Manage and delete cookies and site data
  • Safari (iPhone): Settings › Apps › Safari › Prevent Cross-Site Tracking

The notes stay readable with cookies blocked.

3. Hosting and access logs

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Visits may leave access logs, including IP addresses, on GitHub's servers, which GitHub handles under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs. The site has no visitor analytics of its own; we see only the ad network's aggregate reports.

4. Email

The inquiry boxes in the notes are links that open your mail app; the site stores nothing. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and delete them once the matter is closed.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the effective date on this page and describe the change.
