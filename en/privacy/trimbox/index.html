TrimBox Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Policy version 1.0.0

This policy applies to TrimBox (Korean name 트림박스, package name com.sugarmount.trimbox), an Android simulator for packing camping gear into a car trunk, published by simpest under the developer name SugarMount (“we”, “us”). It describes, based on the app's design and implementation, what data the app handles and what it does not.

1. Summary

The app does not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos, and there is no sign-up or sign-in. The app shows Google AdMob ads, and the ads SDK sends data such as the advertising ID and IP address to Google (section 2). From version 1.0.0 you can buy Pro (a one-time purchase) through Google Play billing, which stops ads and makes saves unlimited; Google Play handles the payment (section 3). Only the ads SDK and Google Play billing use the internet; your packing layouts and their names never leave the device. We run no server and use no usage analytics or crash reporting SDK.

2. Internet use and ads

The internet is used for ads and Google Play billing only

  • The app has the INTERNET permission. The Google Mobile Ads SDK uses it to load ads and show the ad consent screen. Pro purchases and purchase checks go through the Google Play app on your device (section 3).
  • Trunk shapes, the gear list, auto-packing and the “Will it fit?” check are all built into the app and computed on your device. Checking, packing and saving keep working without an internet connection; ads just don't appear and Pro can't be bought then.
  • We run no server. There is no account or sign-in, and no usage analytics (such as Google Analytics) or crash reporting (such as Crashlytics) library.
  • When you tap the privacy policy link in the app, your phone's browser opens this page. That connection is made by the browser.

Ad formats

The app shows ads with Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). It uses no mediation (no other ad networks). Ads appear only in these three formats, and never while you choose gear, auto-pack, run the “Will it fit?” check or follow the loading steps:

  • An app open ad that may appear when you return to the app after being away for a while (at most 2 a day)
  • Rewarded ads that play only when you choose them (“Watch an ad to save & share card”, “Watch an ad to add a slot”)
  • One banner ad at the bottom of the My layouts page

Ad content is limited to the PG rating. With Pro, none of these ads are shown, and when you open the app as a Pro user the ads SDK is not started, so there are no ad requests and no consent screen.

Data the ads SDK collects and shares

To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the ads SDK automatically collects the following data under Google's policies, and shares it with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS).

  • Device or other IDs: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location: the IP address, which may be used to estimate the device's general location (country or region). The app requests no location permission, so precise location such as GPS is not available to the ads SDK.
  • App activity (app interactions): app launches and interactions with ads such as impressions, taps and video views
  • App info and performance (diagnostics): launch time, hangs, errors and performance of the app and the ads SDK

Google collects and processes this data; we have no direct access to it. Your packing layouts, layout names and gear lists are never put into ad requests. For the exact data and purposes, please see Google's information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

Consent and changing your choices

  • The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), it shows an ad privacy consent screen before loading ads. You can use the app without consenting.
  • Where consent is required, Privacy options appears on the About screen, and you can change your consent choices there at any time. It does not appear where it is not required.
  • In Android Settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads), you can reset or delete your advertising ID. Once deleted, it is not used for personalized ads.
  • Rewarded ads play only when you tap the button. If you don't, no rewarded ad is shown. If an ad can't be loaded, the card is made anyway, and the save goes ahead without adding a slot.

3. Pro purchase (Google Play billing)

  • Pro is a one-time purchase (product trimbox_pro) made through Google Play billing. It is not a subscription and nothing renews.
  • The payment screen and payment details such as your payment method, name and billing address are handled by Google Play and are not shared with us. The app never receives card numbers or other payment method details.
  • From Google Play the app receives only the product details (the price in your local currency) and the purchase state (product ID, whether payment is complete or pending, and a purchase token). The purchase token is used only to send Google Play the purchase acknowledgement, and is not stored.
  • Once payment is complete, the app turns Pro on and stores a single Pro yes/no value in the app's storage on your phone, so that Pro keeps working offline. A pending payment does not turn Pro on.
  • When you open the app, when you return to it, and when you tap Restore purchase in About, the app asks Google Play for this account's purchases to keep the Pro state in sync. A refunded or cancelled purchase turns Pro off at the next check. After reinstalling or on a new device, tap Restore purchase with the same Google account.
  • Refunds: email sugarmount21@gmail.com within 7 days of purchase and we will refund you, no questions asked. Within 48 hours of purchase you can also request a refund directly in Google Play. We only need the Google Play order number and ask for no other personal information.
  • Purchase history and refund processing follow Google Play's policies and procedures. Google Play Terms of Service

4. Data stored on your device

  • Auto-save: the layout you are working on, shown again when you reopen the app.
  • Named layouts: layouts you saved under a name, with the time saved, the vehicle and the number of items. You can delete them one by one in My layouts (the load screen). A layout holds up to 64 items.
  • App settings: display language (system, Korean or English) and whether you have seen the first-run guide.
  • Ad records: so that ads do not appear too often, the day's ad counts, the times of the last full-screen ad and the last app open ad, and the number of times the app has been opened, as plain numbers. The number of save slots you unlocked by watching ads is stored here too (1 free, up to 5). Your ad consent status is stored on the device by Google UMP.
  • Pro state: section 3.
  • Error log: section 6.
  • This data lives in Android's app-private storage (SharedPreferences), which other apps cannot read. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. Save slots unlocked with ads are lost too; Pro can be brought back with Restore purchase.

5. What leaves the device only when you tap it

Each of these happens only when you tap a button, and goes through Android's share sheet, file picker or your mail app. What the receiving app or service does with it is governed by its own policy.

  • Save & share card: one card image is created with the trunk layout, the numbered list of item names, the vehicle, the app name, the app's tagline and its Google Play address, and, as you choose, handed to another app through the Android share sheet or saved to your photo storage. When you share, a short text goes with it: the vehicle, the fill rate and volume, each item's name and size, the app's tagline and hashtags, and this app's Google Play link. The temporary file sits in the app's private cache folder, and files older than 10 minutes are removed the next time a share file is created.
  • Referral tag in the shared link: the Google Play link in the shared text ends with utm_source=trimbox_share&utm_campaign=card. It only marks that a visit to the Google Play page came from a shared card, and contains nothing that identifies you, your device or your layout. We see visits and installs per tag only as totals in the Google Play Console.
  • Save to photos: on Android 10 and later, saved as a new image file in your photo storage (Pictures/TrimBox), with no storage permission; the app does not read your other photos. On Android 7 to 9 the share sheet opens instead of asking for a permission.
  • Export and import layouts: layouts (vehicle, second-row seat position, item names, sizes and positions) are saved or opened as JSON files through Android's system file picker. You choose where to save and which file to open, and the app can access only that one file. Files larger than 2 MB are not opened.
  • Feedback email: tapping “Send feedback” on the About screen opens your mail app with the recipient (sugarmount21@gmail.com), a subject (app name and version, device model and Android API level) and diagnostics in the body (app version, device model and Android API level, app display language, and the number of items on screen) filled in. Item names and layout names are not included. The error log is added only if you turn on “Attach error log” (off by default, section 6), and nothing is sent until you tap send in your mail app. The “Request a vehicle” email works the same way, with only the recipient, a subject and a short prompt filled in.
  • If there is no mail app: the app copies our email address to your phone's clipboard and, if you ask, the subject and body too. The clipboard is not sent anywhere.
  • Open Google Play: opens this app's store page in the Google Play app (or the browser if Play is missing), for example to rate the app.

6. Error log

  • When an error occurs, the app keeps the technical details needed to fix it (error type, the first line of the error message, the top lines of the stack trace that point to the code location, the time in UTC and how many times the same error repeated) in the app's storage on your phone. Only the latest 50 entries are kept; older ones are deleted.
  • So that nothing you typed, such as layout names, ends up in the log, text in quotes, words that look like file paths, addresses or email addresses, words with non-English letters (such as Korean) and numbers of five or more digits are masked with “…” before saving.
  • The error log is never sent automatically. It goes into the body of a feedback email, with the app version, build type and app display language (up to 8,000 characters), only if you turn on “Attach error log”, and you can review or delete it in your mail app before sending.

7. Android backup

If Google backup is turned on on your phone, Android may back up this app's stored data (section 4) to your Google account and restore it on a new device. This is a feature of Android and your Google account; we cannot receive or see the backed-up data. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

11. Your rights

  • You can delete the layouts stored on your device in the app, or all of them by clearing app data or uninstalling. We do not hold this data.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.
  • Where consent is required, you can make your ad consent choices again at any time in Privacy options on the About screen.
  • You never have to watch a rewarded ad; it plays only if you tap the button.
  • You can view your Pro purchase and request a refund in Google Play. We hold no payment information.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, delete or stop processing emails you sent us, at the contact below. For data Google controls, such as ad data, we will point you to Google's procedures.

12. Children's privacy

The app is meant for adults, and its target age group on Google Play is 18 and over. It is not directed at children and does not collect age information. If you learn that a child under 14 has emailed us, or if you are a parent or legal guardian requesting deletion, please contact us below. We will delete what we can verify without delay.

15. How we keep personal data safe

  • We run no servers, so no personal data piles up on a server. Layouts, layout names and settings stay in the app's private storage on your phone, which Android keeps other apps from reading.
  • Ad and billing traffic is handled by the Google SDK and the Google Play app over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • The only personal data we receive directly is the email you send us. It stays in a Gmail account only the developer can access; we do not copy it elsewhere or give it to anyone, and we delete it as described in section 10.
  • Before each release, the build is checked automatically for permissions, the advertising ID and data transfers; a build that differs from this policy is not released.
