What this series found

  • Eleven of 19 installs in GA4 came from test devices. Names like sdk_gphone64_arm64 were Google Play pre-launch and review devices, and since the docs say setprop alone does not remove them from reports, we filter them with report exclusions. (Part 1: test devices in GA4)
  • On a fresh install on a Galaxy S21, the block puzzle's first interstitial appeared 91 seconds in, before a single real puzzle: the three tutorial puzzles had met the "three games" rule. Excluding them moved the first interstitial on the emulator from 181 to 314 seconds. (Part 2: interstitial frequency)
  • Adding screen recording to our store-screenshot automation produced 24 images and 6 videos for an app campaign. The second cut, 22.7 seconds long, needed no new recording, only an edited JSON spec. (Part 3: ad assets from gameplay)
  • The best of four first-round campaigns, Block Turn KR, ran a $0.59 CPI at about 3% ROAS, roughly 30 times its break-even CPI. A second round with Block Turn KR alone reached a 7-day ROAS of 8 to 10%, measured a different way. (Part 4: break-even CPI)

This series is for developers of small ad-funded games and apps. Every later decision rests on install counts, so start with test-device filtering in Part 1. If you are adding interstitials, read Part 2; if you plan an app campaign, read Parts 3 and 4 together. The break-even CPI in Part 4 can be worked out before you turn a campaign on, which keeps a cheap-looking CPI from setting your budget.
